# Colab privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026. This policy covers the Colab job service operated by ailuntz at colab-jobs.ailuntz.com.

## Data and purpose

Google supplies an account identifier and OAuth credentials when you connect. The service stores a derived account identifier and encrypted credentials to keep your connection working and isolate your jobs. The service stores submitted Python, parameters, timestamps, job relationships and status, cleanup status, and bounded text output and errors. These records let you track a job, view its output, and rerun it with new parameters.

## Data recipients

Cloudflare hosts the service and stores job and connection data. Google receives credentials for your Google account and the code and parameters needed to run the job in Colab. ChatGPT sends tool requests and receives the status and results that it displays in your conversation. Each service has its own privacy terms for its processing.

## Retention and controls

Job records are kept until you request their removal or until the service removes them or ends. Encrypted Google connection credentials are kept to maintain your connection and are removed from the service on a verified deletion request. You can disconnect the plugin in ChatGPT and revoke the service's access in [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revoking access stops future Google access and does not erase existing job records. For access or deletion requests, [ask for a private follow-up](https://github.com/ailuntx/colab-jobs/issues). Provide account verification only in the private follow-up.
